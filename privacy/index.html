Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how Zaiba Technologies handles information on www.zaibatech.com. Our apps have their own policy: SiraatOne Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

Zaiba Technologies ("ZaibaTech", "we", "us") is an independent software company based in the United States that builds and supports apps, including the SiraatOne apps. We are responsible for the information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us and the minimum needed to run this website:

  • Contact form: your first and last name, email address, topic, subject, message and the language you were using on our site, and, only if you choose to add them, your mobile number and address.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the date and time of your visit, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use it to identify you.
  • Theme and language settings: if you switch between light and dark mode or choose a language, your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage). It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party trackers on this website. Our fonts are stored on our own site, so loading them does not share your information with anyone else.

3. How we use your information

  • To read and reply to your message and give you support.
  • To keep a record of our conversation in case you contact us again.
  • To protect this website and our inbox from spam and abuse.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not send you marketing emails.

4. Service providers

We use a small number of trusted services to run this website. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Web3Forms delivers contact form messages to our email inbox.
  • Zoho Mail hosts our business email, where your messages are received and stored.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the public.

5. Legal basis (EU and UK visitors)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your information based on your consent (the box you tick before sending a message) and our legitimate interest in answering your questions and keeping our services secure. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your messages only as long as needed to answer your request and keep a reasonable record of our conversation. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how we use it. We offer these choices to everyone. To make a request, email privacy@zaibatech.com or use our contact form. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

8. Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

This website uses a secure HTTPS connection, and access to our inbox is limited and protected. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. International visitors

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. By contacting us, you understand that your information may be processed outside your country.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

12. Contact us

For questions about this policy or your information, email privacy@zaibatech.com or use the contact form on our website.
